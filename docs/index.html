Security

Your browser never talks to your services directly.

Every request goes through Novaarr's own backend — which means Novaarr can be the only thing you ever expose to the internet, and everything else stays on your LAN.

01

Passkeys

Sign in with a fingerprint, face, or security key — additional to, not instead of, your PIN or password.

02

Two-factor auth

Standard TOTP, with backup codes, for the shared login or each individual account.

03

Encrypted at rest

Every service credential is AES-256-GCM encrypted in the database, per install.

04

Tailscale & Cloudflare

Reach it over your own tailnet, or a Cloudflare Tunnel — no port-forwarding, no public exposure required.

05

Access roles

Restrict a household member to specific services, widgets, or calendar sources.

06

One-click backup

Export and restore the whole database — your config, not just your media.

Quick start

One container. No separate services to wire up.

Point it at whatever you already run and it appears in the menu — nothing to configure until you say so.

docker-compose.yml 
services:
  novaarr:
    image: therealbatbro/novaarr:latest
    container_name: novaarr
    restart: unless-stopped
    ports:
      - "3210:3000"
    volumes:
      - novaarr-data:/data
    environment:
      SHOW_ALL_SERVICES: "true"

volumes:
  novaarr-data:
